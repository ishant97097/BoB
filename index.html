    FIELD GUIDE

    Take command of Buxar

    Select one of your positions, choose the number of troops, then select Dispatch troops and a connected destination. You can also use Tab and Enter on the map.

    Moving to an enemy position starts a battle. Defenders gain a 2-troop advantage at the fort and 1 elsewhere. Send more troops than the defending strength to capture it.

    Each position can send troops once per turn. Recruit at your selected position; repair while you hold the fort. End turn collects income and lets the enemy advance.

    Win by holding the fort and three positions after 12 turns. Losing the fort ends the campaign.
